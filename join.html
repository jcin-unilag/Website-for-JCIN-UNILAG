What you get

More than a group chat seat.

This year's board has built membership around the IMPACT agenda, so every member has a clear way in and a way up.

1

A structured first month

Meet your directorate, learn one skill, complete one practical task and get feedback at the end of the month.

2

Roles on live projects

Interested members are offered meaningful roles on real projects, then shadow and co-lead with experienced members.

3

Mentorship

Guidance from past directors, project chairs and alumni, through buddy systems and alumni-led sessions.

4

Practical training

Project planning, delegation and reporting, plus career clinics and CV and LinkedIn sessions with partner organisations.

5

Competitions & conventions

Represent UNILAG at inter-chapter competitions and attend local, national and international JCI conventions.

6

A global network

Connect with JCI members across Nigeria and in more than 100 countries, and with alumni who have walked the same path.

Questions

Do I need leadership experience?

No. JCI is built on learning by doing. You'll learn through onboarding, mentorship and working on projects alongside experienced members.

Which directorates can I join?

Our directorates include Programs & Projects, Sponsorship & Partnership, Awards & Documentation, Media & Communications, Membership & Growth and Alumni Affairs. You can tell us your preference when you apply. Confirm full list

How much time does it take?

You choose how involved you get. Some members take on a single role per semester; others lead whole projects. Confirm with Membership & Growth
