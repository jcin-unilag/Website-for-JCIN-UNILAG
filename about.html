One global movement. Our corner of it.

JCI is a worldwide network of young active citizens. JCIN UNILAG is where that network meets the University of Lagos.

How JCI is organised

Global, national, local.

Every member belongs to all three levels at once.

G

JCI (Junior Chamber International)

A global non-profit of young people aged 18 to 40, with members in more than 100 countries and over a century of history.

N

JCI Nigeria

The national organisation that connects local organisations across the country, runs national programmes and represents Nigeria within JCI.

L

JCIN UNILAG

The University of Lagos local organisation, affiliated with JCI Nigeria and JCI. This is where students join, lead projects and grow.

JCI Mission

To provide development opportunities that empower young people to create positive change.

JCI Vision

To be the leading network of young active citizens.

JCIN UNILAG

Close to 40 years on campus.

JCIN UNILAG gives University of Lagos students a place to practise leadership for real. Members plan and run projects, manage budgets and partners, compete with other chapters, and take on board roles, all while still in school.

Each year an elected board sets the agenda. For 2026/27 that agenda is IMPACT: Inclusion and Belonging, Mentorship, Practical Skills Development, Accountability, Community Building and Transformation.

What we're here to do

Our five purposes

From the Constitution of JCI University of Lagos.

  1. Building awareness and acceptance of the responsibilities of citizenship.
  2. Individual participation in internal training to develop leadership potential.
  3. Active participation in planning and running programmes that develop both the individual and the community.
  4. Promoting social and economic development in the community.
  5. Furthering understanding, goodwill and cooperation among all peoples.
How members grow

Four Areas of Opportunity

01

Individual Development

Trainings, mentorship and leading real teams, so members leave with skills they can name.

02

Business & Entrepreneurship

Career clinics, internships and pitching to sponsors, linking campus leadership to the working world.

03

Community Impact

Projects with student clubs, faculties and communities around Lagos that leave lasting change.

04

International Cooperation

Conventions, inter-chapter competitions and a network that reaches across Nigeria and beyond.
